How to use UpscalePress

1. Add pictures

Click ＋ Add pictures, drop pictures on the window or paste one with Ctrl+V. JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP and GIF work; transparent PNGs stay transparent.

2. Choose and upscale

  • Standard – fast, for photos, screenshots and old pictures. Remove noise smooths grain and JPEG blocks.
  • Best detail (Pro) – slower, finer detail for photos.
  • Drawings, anime & logos (Pro) – clean lines and flat colours.

Choose 2×, 3×, 4× or 8× (Pro) and click ✨ Upscale. The first time, the AI model is downloaded once. With a graphics card a photo takes seconds.

3. Compare and save

Drag the white line to compare before and after. Tick Real size (1:1) to look at every pixel and drag to move around. Save as PNG, JPG or WEBP.
