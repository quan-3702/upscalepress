UpscalePress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. UpscalePress is made by NTQ37.

Your pictures stay on your computer

The AI that enlarges your pictures runs on your own computer. Your pictures and the results are stored only in this app on your device. They are never uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else.

What is downloaded

The first time you use a model, the open-source Real-ESRGAN AI model (5 to 67 MB) is downloaded once from Hugging Face, and the AI engine library from jsDelivr. These downloads contain no information about you or your pictures. After that UpscalePress works offline.

Purchases

UpscalePress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
